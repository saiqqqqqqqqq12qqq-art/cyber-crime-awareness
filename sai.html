What is Cyber Crime?

Cyber crime is illegal activity carried out using computers, smartphones, networks or the internet. It can target people's personal information, money, accounts or digital devices.

Common Cyber Crimes

🎣 Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into giving away sensitive information.

💳 Online Scams

Fraudulent offers, fake payments and other tricks used to steal money or information.

🪪 Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without permission.

🦠 Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices or compromise information.

💬 Cyberbullying

Harassment, threats or harmful behaviour through digital platforms.

🔓 Account Theft

Unauthorized access to online accounts such as email or social media.

How To Stay Safe

  • 🔐 Use strong and unique passwords.
  • 📱 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • 🚫 Never share your OTP, PIN or password.
  • 🔗 Check links before clicking them.
  • ⬆️ Keep your phone and apps updated.
  • 👀 Be careful with unknown messages and offers.
