What is Cyber Crime?

Understanding the digital world is the first step toward staying safe.

Cyber crime is illegal activity carried out using computers, smartphones, networks or the internet. It can target personal information, money, online accounts and digital devices.

Common Cyber Threats

Know the threats before they become a problem.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

💳

Online Scams

Fraudulent offers, fake payments and misleading messages used to steal money or information.

🪪

Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without permission.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that may damage devices or compromise information.

💬

Cyberbullying

Harassment or harmful behaviour carried out through digital platforms.

🔓

Account Theft

Unauthorized access to online accounts such as email or social media.

How To Stay Safe

Simple habits can make your digital life safer.

  • 🔐 Use strong and unique passwords.
  • 📱 Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • 🚫 Never share your OTP, PIN or password.
  • 🔗 Check links carefully before clicking.
  • ⬆️ Keep your phone and apps updated.
  • 👀 Be careful with unknown messages and offers.
